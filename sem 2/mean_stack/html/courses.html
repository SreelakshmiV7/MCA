Our History

Infomax Computer Academy (ICA) was established in the year 2000 with the objective of imparting quality computer education to students and professionals. Over the years, ICA has grown to become a leading institution in the field of computer education, known for its comprehensive curriculum and experienced faculty.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to empower individuals with the knowledge and skills needed to excel in the ever-evolving field of technology. We envision a future where every student who walks through our doors is equipped to make meaningful contributions to the tech industry and society at large.
